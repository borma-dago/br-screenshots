What is an attribute, and which facts are attributes at all?

Today title, price and barcode are columns; weight and manufacturer are attributes. What is the rule? Is a definition (name, type, allowed values, owner) a separate thing from a value (one product's answer)? Is brand an attribute or an entity?

Options

  • Universal and read by code → column; category-specific and staff-authored → attribute recommended
  • Everything is an attribute, even the title (Amazon)
  • Only a small fixed set of custom fields beside the columns (Square: 10 visible + 10 hidden)
  • Added by the brief, with reason: one definition registry over both storages — a storage discriminator says column or value row (Magento backend_type='static') take with option 1

Decision: adopt the Shopify Standard Product Taxonomy as our category tree and append our own nodes under it. This is option (b) with local extension; the (a) recommendation below is superseded.

Why this standard: management chose to align with a published standard. Of the two candidates, Google Product Taxonomy has no licence (Google's Terms of Service keep Google's rights), and Shopify's is MIT. GS1 GPC, UNSPSC, ECLASS, ETIM and others were checked on the standards page.

What “append” means — proposed rules:
