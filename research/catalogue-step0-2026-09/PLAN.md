## How the two locks were decided — the method to replicate, per question

1. **Who uses it, who does not, and why** — one cell per platform across the thirteen records, tallied per option with a *named* ambiguous list, weighted by "tried and tested"; unanimity called out as strong evidence, splits marked for elaboration; a **why** column with the vendor's stated rationale cited or the explaining constraint labelled as inference; **plus the card's own research pointer, executed verbatim** (e.g. A0: Square's 10 visible + 10 hidden, Shopify metafield storefront visibility, eBay `aspectUsage`, Magento's `is_visible_on_front` / `is_filterable` / `used_in_product_listing`). Several pointers name sources the thirteen records never collected — Meta's variant-consistency rules (C1), GS1 and BPOM net-content rules (A4), Square's custom-attribute caps (A0), Shopify metafield visibility (A0), Akeneo option order and metric type (A4), eBay `aspectUsage` (A0), Shopify's food-branch depth and attributes per node (B3), Walmart count/multipack fields (C2). **A bounded new-source pass is allowed for exactly the card pointers**, run to the `external-research` skill: primary artifact plus a second independent route, identifiers verbatim, verified or explicitly "not collected + route", no verdicts inside the data. Everything fetched lands in `~/copilot/research/catalogue-step0-2026-09/corpus/<platform>/`, never in a scratchpad or `/tmp`; the corpus is listed in the brief's §7 and left in place.
2. **Which is cleanest / structurally correct** — depth driven by the split, not only the tier: where platforms diverge, each live option is written against our real models at the two pins to surface the problems it causes (tables, write path, staff editor, search, feed, customer page; forced forks and call sites counted; which invariants stay database-enforceable); where they are unanimous, one fit-check. Every question, whatever its page tier, gets the full treatment and a red-team; the page tiers order the synthesis, they do not thin the research.
3. **Our own numbers** — the measurements the card asks for, from BigQuery with a `.sql` file and a date (e.g. "which fields actually differ across the 33 Indomie rows", "count our own x6 / isi 6 titles", "which of our 292 used non-leaf nodes are real types vs catch-alls", "the distinct attribute names our 639 main nodes would need").
4. **Costs per option**, recommendation labelled as judgement, confidence, what would reopen it — and **what the answer forces in steps 1–5** of the Next Steps page (which D-default it confirms or rewrites). No research into the D-mechanics beyond that.
5. **Independent red-team** of every cite, number and tally; corrections kept visible.
6. **Output** — private artifact pages; no GitHub posting and no edits to existing pages until Irvan says so.
